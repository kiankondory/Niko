# Niko — Safety and Privacy

Niko is a cessation support tool, not a replacement for a doctor, therapist, or emergency service.

## Data and consent

- Collect the minimum data required for a stated purpose; define retention for sensitive data.
- Sharing and AI use must be opt-in, revocable, and explainable.
- Protect local and transmitted data with appropriate security; never place secrets in the repository or logs.
- Provide user-controlled export and deletion, with documented sync/backup behavior.
- Keep analytics minimal, preferably aggregate, and free of sensitive content.

## Product safety

- Coach content must be non-judgmental, non-diagnostic, and explicit about its limits.
- For crisis or danger signals, direct users to appropriate local professional/emergency services; do not hard-code unverified locations or numbers.
- AI must not claim medical certainty, prescribe treatment, or predict relapse with certainty.
- Every new feature requires threat modeling, abuse cases, permission review, and a disable/rollback path.
